# Grand Lake Theater scene sources

The theater and nearby blocks use a surveyed local frame centered near 3200 Grand Avenue. The source grid uses 0.54 scene units per ground meter. The camera looks toward the corner entrance from the intersection side. The mapped theater footprint spans about 24 by 36 scene units, with the main roof near 10.8 units above the street datum.

- The terrain and sampled roof heights come from a clip of about 2.8 million points in the upgraded 2019 QL1 collection. Its EPT collection name is `CA_AlamedaCo_2_2021`. The clip covered about 226 m by 226 m around Oakland’s landmark marker. Ground-class returns supplied the grid, and non-ground returns inside mapped footprints supplied approximate roof heights. [USGS EPT metadata](https://s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/usgs-lidar-public/CA_AlamedaCo_2_2021/ept.json) · [NOAA collection record](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/71712)
- The theater outline, sign alignment, nearby building footprints, and road centerlines use © OpenStreetMap contributors. The OpenStreetMap-derived data in `tools/grand-lake-site.json` is available under ODbL. The rendered street widths, markings, trees, and material colors are stylized. [OpenStreetMap map data for the area](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=-122.249,37.810,-122.246,37.813) · [OpenStreetMap attribution and license](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright)
- The arched facade details, cream and red curved marquee, and roof sign are modeled by hand, using Oakland’s historic-landmark photos and the mapped alignment. The background building volumes and roof heights are approximations. A camera-side block is omitted from the render so the marquee and intersection remain visible. [City of Oakland landmark record](https://www.oaklandca.gov/Planning-Building/Historic-Preservation/List-of-Designated-Landmarks/Grand-Lake-Theater-and-Roof-Sign)

The checked-in JSON contains the small scene-frame extract used by Blender. Run `blender --background --python tools/build-scenes.py -- theatre --blend` to regenerate the editable scene and GLB. Regenerate the desktop and portrait stills with `node tools/capture-posters.mjs theatre rain-night` while the local server is running.
