# Weather Miniatures

A weather display that pairs a location's current conditions with an animated miniature scene.

## Language

**Landmark scene**:
A stylized miniature of a specific landmark, initially the Golden Gate Bridge or Grand Lake Theater.

**Generic landscape**:
A reusable miniature scene that represents the weather at a searched location.

**Selected location**:
The place whose current weather is represented in the scene.

**Landmark preset**:
A choice that pairs a named landmark scene with the place where the landmark stands.

**Searched location**:
A place chosen from search results whose weather is represented in the generic landscape.

**Scene weather**:
The selected location's weather category and local day or night state as represented in a miniature scene.

**Neutral scene**:
The baseline look of the miniature when weather effects or time-of-day lighting are unavailable.
